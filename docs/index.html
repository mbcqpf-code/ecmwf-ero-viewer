ECMWF IFS Day 1–5 ERO Viewer — Product Guide

🌧️ 3-Hr QPF (24-hr Max 3-Hourly Rate — 0.25° ENS & 9 km DET)

0.5", 1.0", 1.5", 2.0": 50-km (~2 grid-cell) neighborhood probability that a member's peak 3-hourly rainfall rate exceeds the threshold during the 12Z–12Z synoptic window.
90th Pct: 90th percentile of the 50-km neighborhood maximum 3-hourly QPF across all 50 members (reasonable worst-case intensity ceiling).
9km Det: High-resolution 0.1° (~9 km) deterministic IFS 24-hour maximum 3-hourly rainfall rate. Disseminated ~2 hours after the 0.25° ensemble and backfilled automatically.

⚡ MUCAPE (24-hr Max Instability)

Mean & 90th Pct: Ensemble mean and 90th percentile of each member's 24-hour maximum Most Unstable CAPE (J/kg), highlighting both surface-based warm sectors and elevated nocturnal instability.

💧 PWAT (Raw Inches & ERA5 1950–2025 Climatological Percentiles)

PWAT (in) — Mean & 90th Pct: Ensemble mean and 90th percentile 24-hour maximum Precipitable Water (inches).
PWAT Climo — Mean Rank & 90th Rank: Exact historical percentile rank (75th–100th) of the forecast PW plume compared against 76 years (1950–2025) of ERA5 daily max PW within a 15-day moving calendar window.
PWAT Climo — Prob >90th & Prob >99th: Percentage of ensemble members exceeding the local climatological 90th or 99th percentile PW threshold.

🌬️ 850 hPa Moisture Flux Convergence & Transport

Mean & 90th Pct: Time-integrated positive 850 hPa Moisture Flux Convergence (g/kg) over 24 hours, overlaid with 24-hour vector-mean moisture transport (qV) arrows and magenta contours where ≥30% / ≥60% of members sustain strong convergent transport (|qV| ≥ 150 g/kg·m/s and MFC ≥ 0.5 g/kg/hr) for ≥9 hours—highlighting anchored LLJ noses favorable for training/backbuilding.

🔥 850 hPa CAPE Flux Convergence & Transport

Mean & 90th Pct: Time-integrated positive 850 hPa CAPE Flux Convergence (-∇·(MUCAPE × V850), in kJ/kg) over 24 hours, overlaid with 24-hour vector-mean instability transport arrows (MUCAPE × V850). Isolates zones where low-level flow advects upstream instability into a lifting boundary while filtering out stable-to-unstable gradients.

🌿 Env Composite (Pure Environmental Index — FFEI, No QPF)

What It Measures: A QPF-independent 24-hr (12Z–12Z) environmental composite computed member-by-member from raw PWAT, ERA5 PWAT anomaly, 24-hr integrated 850 hPa Moisture Flux Convergence (+ persistence), and 24-hr integrated 850 hPa CAPE Flux Convergence. Highlights favorable synoptic/mesoscale ingredients even where parameterized model QPF under-performs.
Value Scale (General Guidance): < 0.50 = marginal/isolated ingredients; 0.50–1.00 = developing or localized overlap; 1.00–2.00 = notable multi-ingredient overlap; 2.00–3.00 = high-end overlap typical of organized training/LLJ MCS setups; ≥ 3.00 = extreme environmental alignment.

🎯 FF Composite (Full Flash Flood Ingredient Index — FFI, With QPF)

What It Measures: Blends normalized environmental ingredients (60% weight: PWAT rarity/depth, MUCAPE, and 24-hr integrated 850 hPa moisture convergence + persistence) with 50-km 3-hr QPF (40% weight) across each 24-hr (12Z–12Z) window. Uses the same 0.50 / 1.00 / 2.00 / 3.00+ severity scale as the Env Composite.
