🌧️ 24-hr Max 3-hr QPF (0.25° 50-Member Ensemble & 9 km Deterministic)

0.5", 1.0", 1.5", 2.0" 3h Probabilities: 50-km (~2 grid-cell) neighborhood probability that a member's peak 3-hourly rainfall rate exceeds the threshold during the 12Z–12Z synoptic window.
90th QPF: 90th percentile of the 50-km neighborhood maximum 3-hourly QPF across all 50 members (reasonable worst-case intensity ceiling).
9km 3h: High-resolution 0.1° (~9 km) deterministic IFS 24-hour maximum 3-hourly rainfall rate. Disseminated ~2 hours after the 0.25° ensemble and backfilled automatically.

⚡ Convective Instability (MUCAPE)

CAPE Mean & CAPE 90th: Ensemble mean and 90th percentile of each member's 24-hour maximum Most Unstable CAPE (J/kg), highlighting both surface-based warm sectors and elevated nocturnal instability.

💧 Precipitable Water & ERA5 1950–2025 Climatological Percentiles

PW Mean & PW 90th: Ensemble mean and 90th percentile 24-hour maximum Precipitable Water (inches).
PW Pct (Mean) & PW Pct (90th): Exact historical percentile rank (75th–100th) of the forecast PW plume compared against 76 years (1950–2025) of ERA5 daily max PW within a 15-day moving calendar window.
Prob >90th & Prob >99th: Percentage of ensemble members exceeding the local climatological 90th or 99th percentile PW threshold.

🌬️ 850 hPa Moisture Transport Convergence & Persistence

850 MFC Mean & 90th: Time-integrated positive 850 hPa Moisture Flux Convergence (g/kg) over 24 hours, overlaid with 24-hour vector-mean moisture transport (qV) arrows and magenta contours where ≥30% / ≥60% of members sustain strong convergent transport (|qV| ≥ 150 g/kg·m/s and MFC ≥ 0.5 g/kg/hr) for ≥9 hours—highlighting anchored LLJ noses favorable for training/backbuilding.
